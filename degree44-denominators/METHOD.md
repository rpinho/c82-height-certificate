Baseline and credit: Doche's limit-point formula; Grinsztajn/Doche factors; Épi's Ed5 and unequal multiplicities; Pinho's certificate, joint LP and dual lattices; vidigoat PR #280's iterated numerator IRLS/LLL, public code pinned in PRIOR_ART.md. The denominator price for a monic integer R of degree d is

    rc(R) = log M(R o T) - sum_k nu_k log|R(T(exp(2*pi*i*t_k)))| - lambda*d.

Here nu is the nonnegative dual measure of the positive-part epigraph constraints. The continuous Mahler term is the uniform-circle log integral, so the search attempts to minimize the logarithmic average against the complementary measure (uniform minus nu). A stratified discrete quadrature of that measure drives the heuristic; final reduced costs use complete FLINT root enclosures converted to float, and final certificates use ball arithmetic throughout.

Unconstrained monic IRLS often collapses onto products of X, X-1 and P4. Those have small height but conflict with necessary numerator factors. The effective search fixes residues:

    R = R_start + H*U, H = X*(X-1)*P4, deg U < deg R_start - 6.

This preserves R(0), R(1) and R mod P4. A starting product of known denominators is monic and coprime to H. A lattice with one strongly penalized anchor coordinate fixes the leading coefficient to 1; the remaining coordinates encode integer coefficients of U. LLL reduces its weighted evaluation norm. IRLS reweights evaluation points by 1/|R_current|^2, then selects by the log average. Small combinations of short rows explore nearby integer polynomials. All proposed factors are exactly factored and checked for coprimality against the opposite library.
